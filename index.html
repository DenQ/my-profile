AI call analysis & streaming scoring

Sivrosa · Tech Lead Fullstack

The business needed to quickly automate scoring of audio calls, find target leads, and extract insights — on a Go + Angular stack.

Under a tight deadline I picked up Angular and rebuilt the frontend on Feature-Sliced Design. Designed a Go backend for high-load streaming audio analysis through Yandex Cloud (YandexGPT / SpeechKit).

Shipped the AI solution to production — months of manual scoring and build work taken off the team, with automatic lead qualification wired into end-to-end business logic.

2014 04

A portal that filled itself

Garage Media · Fullstack architect

The brief was an autonomous video portal from scratch. No architecture. No content. No video. Just a deadline and an empty screen.

First I sketched the skeleton and the architecture on PHP, Yii2, PostgreSQL. Then Kinopoisk and IMDb parsers — and a catalog appeared. Then bots that hit torrents, downloaded in parallel, and converted to MP4. In the end only SEO copy needed human edits — controllable, on purpose.

The service became self-sufficient: parse, fill, download, convert, stream. Content managers almost optional — only light edits where the machine still can’t.

PHP · Yii2 · PostgreSQL · RabbitMQ
